# Code of conduct

crater is built by people in their free time. This page says how we treat each other in its issues, pull requests, discussions, the crater registry and any chat about the project.

## Expected

- Be respectful, also when you disagree. Criticize code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent. Many people here write English as a second language, and many are new to Luau or Roblox.
- Keep reports and reviews useful: what happened, what you expected, how to reproduce it.
- Accept a "no" on a feature or a pull request; ask why if it is not clear.
- Credit the work of others.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, threats, slurs, and personal or political attacks.
- Sexual content, or attention someone has asked you to stop.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as their address, real name or private messages, without their permission.
- Spam, advertising, and flooding issues or pull requests.
- Publishing packages with backdoors, malware or stolen code, or trying to get them past registry review.
- Impersonating other people or projects, or registering scopes to squat their names.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer on Discord: send a direct message to `@__ncall`. Include links or screenshots of what happened. Reports are kept private, and the person who reports is not named to the other side without their permission.

Security problems and malicious packages have their own process, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## What happens

Depending on what happened, a maintainer may:

1. ask the person to stop, privately or in the thread
2. edit, hide or delete comments, close issues and pull requests
3. deprecate or remove packages from the crater registry and refuse new versions from that scope
4. block the person from the repositories, temporarily or for good

Serious cases, like threats or malware, can skip straight to the last step.

## Scope

This applies in every space of the project: the [crater](https://github.com/TheExeDEV/crater) and [crater-registry](https://github.com/TheExeDEV/crater-registry) repositories, the package website, and chats where people talk about crater. It also applies outside them when someone acts on behalf of the project.
